Pizza Recipe

About this recipe

This is a simple pizza recipe.

I based this off of this project.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni

Instructions

  1. Get the ingredients.
  2. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  3. Spread the sauce and toppings on the dough.
  4. Bake for 12 minutes.
