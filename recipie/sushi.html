Sushi Recipe

About this recipe

This is a simple sushi roll recipe.

Ingredients

  • Sushi rice
  • Rice vinegar
  • Nori (seaweed sheets)
  • Sushi-grade salmon
  • Cucumber
  • Avocado
  • Soy sauce

Instructions

  1. Get the ingredients.
  2. Cook the rice and mix in the rice vinegar.
  3. Spread the rice on a sheet of nori and add the fillings.
  4. Roll it up tightly, slice it, and serve with soy sauce.
