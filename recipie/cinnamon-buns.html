Cinnamon Buns Recipe

About this recipe

This is a simple cinnamon buns recipe.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Brown sugar
  • Cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Get the ingredients.
  2. Mix the dough and let it rise.
  3. Roll out the dough and spread the cinnamon filling.
  4. Roll it up, slice, and bake for 25 minutes.
